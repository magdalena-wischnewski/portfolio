About

Researcher in psychology, media psychology & human–AI interaction

Magdalena Wischnewski examines how people understand, trust, evaluate, and rely on artificial intelligence systems — especially in contexts where technology, uncertainty, communication, and social identity intersect.

Her academic background includes a PhD in Psychology from the University of Duisburg-Essen, completed summa cum laude, with a thesis on misinformation on social media and motivated reasoning. Her work spans AI trust, misinformation, explainable AI, AI certification, anthropomorphism, and the social perception of automated systems.

Research Areas

What I study

◆

Trust in AI

How users calibrate trust in automated systems, certifications, AI labels, system performance, and explainable AI.

✦

Misinformation & Motivated Reasoning

How identity, emotion, belief, and prior opinion shape engagement with misinformation and polarizing news.

◉

Human–AI Communication

How language, mentalistic framing, anthropomorphism, and public communication affect perceptions of AI and LLMs.

■

Trustworthy AI Systems

How AI certification, governance, labels, and user-facing communication can support responsible AI development.
